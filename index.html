📈 The Evolution & Burning Cycle

Dragon Prime utilizes a deflationary burn mechanic. To climb the ranks and achieve higher-tier power, players must sacrifice multiple copies of lower-tier assets. This keeps the ecosystem thriving and rewards dedicated collectors.
