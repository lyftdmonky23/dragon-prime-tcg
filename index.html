Realm Lore & Origins

The history of the primordial forge.

In the primordial era of the WAX realm, raw elemental energy condensed into crystalline shards known as Mysterious Dragon Essence. Alchemists discovered that when this essence is subjected to extreme heat in the fusion crucible, it crystallizes into metallic containment shells—the legendary Dragon Eggs.

Forged through meticulous tier-based blending, these eggs hatch into formidable dragon wyrms. Each progressive tier—from the steadfast Bronze to the mythic Platinum apex—requires immense sacrifice, precise compounding, and unwavering dedication from tamers across the blockchain.

1. Acquire & Unbox Essence

Mysterious Dragon Essence is priced at a micro-transaction friendly 0.01 WAX so anyone can enter the market. Purchase your Essence packs directly via AtomicHub Drops, then burn 3x Essence in NeftyBlocks to roll for random metallic eggs.

2. Fuse & Upgrade Tiers

Utilize NeftyBlocks Blends to combine 3 matching eggs or dragons. Higher tiers command staggering valuations, rewarding early adopters and master alchemists who manage the scarce Platinum supply.
