1. Ecosystem Overview

2. Evolution & Synthesis Loop

Every tier progression utilizes strict burn mechanics. Lower-tier assets are permanently removed from circulation to mint higher-tier dragons.

1. Primary Acquisition

Mysterious Dragon Essence

Price: 2.00 WAX (Infinite Supply)

Base raw reagent powering the entire economy.

2. Egg Incubation

Dragon Egg

Recipe: Burn 4x Essence

Transforms raw essence into a dormant egg.

3. Dragon Awakening

Bronze Dragon

Recipe: Burn 3x Dragon Eggs

Hatches your first active metallic dragon.

4. Silver Evolution

Silver Dragon

Recipe: Burn 3x Bronze Dragons

Combines three bronze dragons into a silver grade dragon.

5. Gold Evolution

Gold Dragon

Recipe: Burn 3x Silver Dragons

Evolves three silver dragons into a high-tier gold dragon.

6. Apex Evolution CAP: 1,000

Platinum Dragon

Recipe: Burn 3x Gold Dragons

Peak draconic form. Hard capped at 1,000 total mints worldwide.
